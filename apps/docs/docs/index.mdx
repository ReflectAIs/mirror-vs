# Mirror VS Docs

Imagine an AI coding agent that doesn't just chat — it *does stuff*. It reads files, runs terminal commands, navigates your project, and autonomously works through multi-step workflows. That's Mirror VS.

It's open-source, model-agnostic (no vendor lock-in here), and designed to wring every drop of potential out of today's large language models. We believe in trading tokens for quality — and it shows.

## What Mirror VS can do for you

- **Generate code** from plain English descriptions
- **Refactor & debug** like a seasoned pro
- **Write & update** documentation (meta, we know)
- **Answer questions** about your codebase — even the messy parts
- **Automate the boring stuff** so you can focus on the fun stuff
- **Tap into MCP Servers** to extend its capabilities even further

## The Mirror VS mindset

The developers who get the most out of Mirror VS tend to share a few traits:

- **Embrace model agnosticism:** Mirror VS doesn't *contain* AI — it *connects* to it. Swap between dozens of [LLM providers](/providers) and hundreds of models. The "best model" changes every other week anyway; we've got you covered.

- **Don't be stingy with tokens:** Fancy state-of-the-art models with generous token budgets almost always beat cheap models running on a shoestring. Yes, keep an eye on costs. But remember: tokens are cheaper than your time.

- **Trust the roles:** Customizable [Modes](/basic-usage/using-modes) (Architect, Code, Debug, etc.) keep the AI focused and on-task. Let the Architect architect, let the Coder code — that's why they're called modes, not "vague suggestions."

- **Be ambitious — seriously:** Mirror VS works *better* the more you trust it. Start with the hand-holding (everything requires approval), then gradually enable [Auto-Approve](/features/auto-approving-actions) as you build confidence. Before you know it, you'll be throwing full-stack rewrites at the [Orchestrator](/basic-usage/using-modes#orchestrator-mode-aka-boomerang-mode) and watching the magic unfold.
