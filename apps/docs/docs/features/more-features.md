# Additional Features

This page rounds up the extra goodies in Mirror VS that make your development workflow smoother, faster, and maybe even a little more fun. Consider it the "and much more" section of a late-night infomercial — except all of this actually works.

---

## Suggested Responses

Mirror VS provides suggested responses to questions, saving you time typing. These suggestions appear as buttons below the chat input box after you ask a question. Click a suggestion to quickly use it as your next prompt.

This feature aims to streamline your workflow by anticipating your potential follow-up questions and providing one-click access to relevant prompts. It's like having a mind reader, but one that you trained.

---

## Text to Speech

Mirror VS includes a Text-to-Speech (TTS) feature that reads out the AI responses, allowing you to listen to the information instead of reading it. This can be helpful for accessibility, learning, or simply for a change of pace.

To use Text-to-Speech, simply enable it in the Mirror VS settings. Once enabled, a speaker icon will appear next to each AI response in the chat. Click the icon to start listening. Perfect for those moments when you want to close your eyes and pretend your AI is a podcast host.

---

## Global Language Support

Mirror VS supports 18 languages, making it accessible to a wider range of users globally. You can now use Mirror VS in:

- English
- Simplified Chinese
- Traditional Chinese
- Spanish
- Hindi
- French
- Portuguese
- German
- Japanese
- Korean
- Italian
- Turkish
- Vietnamese
- Polish
- Catalan
- Russian
- Dutch
- Indonesian

To change your language, go to **Advanced Settings > Language** in the Mirror VS settings panel.

This global update ensures a smoother and more inclusive coding experience for users around the world. Because great code is universal, and so should be the tool that helps you write it.
